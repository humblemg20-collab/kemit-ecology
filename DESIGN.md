# KEMIT ECOLOGY — Design Context

## Direction

KEMIT is a documentary industrial brand: blackened biomass, honest production photography, precise typography and quiet commercial confidence. The page should feel like a material archive that also makes the next action obvious.

Signature: a full-bleed obsidian hero with oversized split-line typography and a thin vertical "Matière → possibilité" marker that carries through the homepage.

## Tokens

- Obsidian: `#080808`
- Carbon: `#1A1A1A`
- Graphite: `#363636`
- Steel: `#858585`
- Silver: `#D9D9D9`
- Ivory: `#F5F5F3`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

Typography uses a high-contrast editorial serif for display headlines, Inter for readable body copy, and condensed uppercase utility labels for navigation and process markers.

## Layout rules

- Alternate immersive black sections and clean ivory/white editorial sections.
- Use asymmetrical grids, hairline borders and restrained radii; photography keeps its natural color.
- Primary actions remain explicit: discover products, request a professional quote, or open WhatsApp.
- Preserve the hash SPA routes and the central product catalogue.

## Motion and accessibility

- Reveal sections with opacity/translate transitions only when reduced motion is not requested.
- Never lock document scrolling or move the primary CTA during loading.
- Keep visible focus states, semantic controls and WCAG AA contrast.
